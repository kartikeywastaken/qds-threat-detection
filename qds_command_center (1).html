Statistical Tests & Mathematical Bounds

Minimum Entropy Bound (Quantum-Secured)

The system evaluates the real-time min-entropy bound to ensure that an eavesdropper cannot guess the teleported signature. When the CHSH parameter drops below 2.0 (classical bound), the evaluated min-entropy falls to 0.

SPRT Decision Boundary

The Sequential Probability Ratio Test monitors the log-likelihood ratio of an honest channel vs. an intercept-resend attack. The upper bound is fixed at 4.60 (Reject) and lower bound at -4.60 (Accept).
